The essentials

  • 62 Medicare Advantage plans are on offer in Orange County for 2027, from 15 companies. 54 charge no monthly premium (you still pay Part B).
  • 20 of the county's 2026 plans end, merge or split for 2027: 16 end, 3 merge and 1 splits. If your carrier sent you a letter, we'll go over your situation at no cost.
  • Annual enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 (2027 changes). Whatever you pick takes effect January 1.

Questions people ask us

Are you the government? Is this a scam?

No and no. We are a licensed family agency, not Medicare. Check Alejandra's license #4507923 at the California Department of Insurance. Medicare won't call you asking for your number. If someone does, hang up.

Does your help cost anything? Who pays you?

It is free for you. The insurance company pays us. You get the comparison, the paperwork and our help all year.

My plan sent a letter saying it is ending. What do I do?

Plans send these letters every fall. Call us before December 7: we compare the options in your county against your medicines and your doctor, and we handle the switch.

Can I keep my doctor if I change plans?

It depends on the plan, because each one has its own directory. Before you switch, we look up your doctor and clinic in the 2027 directory and confirm it for you.
