## Not done this pass

- **Favicon/font `<link>` stamping**: families identified (`partials/fav--*.html`,
  `partials/font--*.html` exist as reference, 2 + 6 families) but **not wired into any
  page**. Caught a real bug while building this: an early version of the stamp script
  relocated the GA4 script block to just before `</head>` instead of leaving it in its
  original position — caught by reading the actual diff before committing (not by the
  gate), reverted, and GA4 was fixed to replace in place. Favicon/font tags are more
  scattered across `<head>` (not reliably contiguous), so the same risk applies more
  broadly; rather than rebuild and re-verify that under today's push, left as a listed
  follow-up. 24 pages currently have no favicon `<link>` at all — none gained one.
- **Color-token substitution**: `partials/brand-tokens.css` ships as reference only, not
  wired into any page's inline `<style>`. Checked MC's own widget-host pages first, per
  the parallel BM finding: **`index.html` and `es.html` — the two pages that host the
  plan-finder widget iframe — each define their own `--navy:#0B2D4F`**, different from the
  sitewide brand navy. 9 other pages also carry their own `:root` block
  (`beneficios.html dual-eligibility-medicare-medi-cal.html benefits.html careers.html
  404.html contact.html how-to-enroll-medicare-california.html
  medicare-costs-california.html contacto.html open-enrollment-california.html`).
  Substituting hex-for-var sitewide would silently repaint whichever of these actually
  differ from the token values — not attempted.
