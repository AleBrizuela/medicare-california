Extra Benefits of Medicare Advantage in California: Dental, Vision and More in 2026

One of the most common questions we hear is: "What else does Medicare Advantage cover beyond the basics?" The answer may surprise you. In addition to covering everything Original Medicare covers, many Medicare Advantage plans in California include extra benefits that simply do not exist in the federal program: dental, vision, hearing, gym memberships, rides to medical appointments, and more. In many cases, these benefits come with plans that charge $0 per month in premium.

This article walks through each of these benefits, what you can realistically expect from them, and how to make sure you choose the plan that best fits your life.

Why Does Original Medicare Not Cover These?

Original Medicare, created in 1965, was designed mainly to cover hospitalizations and doctor visits. Dental care, eyeglasses, hearing aids, and preventive wellness were left out of that original design. Over time, some of these benefits became available through Medicare Advantage plans, which are administered by private insurers approved by Medicare and have flexibility to add benefits beyond what the federal program covers.

To understand how Medicare Advantage fits into the broader Medicare picture, visit our guide on Medicare Advantage plans.

The Most Common Extra Benefits in California

Dental Coverage

Dental is the most frequently asked-about extra benefit. The key thing to understand is that not all plans offer the same level of dental coverage. There are typically three tiers:

  • Basic preventive: Two cleanings per year, routine x-rays, and a dental exam. This level is the most common and is available in most Medicare Advantage plans in California, many with $0 premium.
  • Basic comprehensive: In addition to preventive services, covers fillings, extractions, and treatment of gum disease. The plan may have an annual benefit maximum of $500 to $1,500.
  • Enhanced comprehensive: Covers crowns, bridges, dentures, and in some cases dental implants. Annual maximums can reach $3,000 or more. These plans may carry a somewhat higher monthly premium.

Practical tip: Before enrolling in a plan for its dental benefit, confirm that your current dentist is in the plan's network. If not, you may have to choose between staying with your dentist or using the new dental benefit.

Vision Benefit

Most Medicare Advantage plans in California include a vision benefit that covers:

  • An annual comprehensive eye exam (including refraction)
  • A dollar allowance toward eyeglasses or frames (typically $100 to $300 per year)
  • Access to optical networks such as VSP, EyeMed, or similar

Some higher-tier plans also cover contact lenses as an alternative to eyeglasses. Original Medicare does not cover routine eye exams or corrective lenses, so this benefit can represent immediate savings for many beneficiaries.

Hearing Benefit

Hearing aids are expensive. Without insurance, a pair can cost between $2,000 and $7,000. Some Medicare Advantage plans in California include a hearing benefit that covers:

  • An annual hearing exam
  • A significant discount or a dollar maximum toward hearing aids (for example, $500 to $2,500 per pair)
  • Access to a network of participating audiologists

Not every plan includes a hearing benefit, and those that do vary considerably in how generous the coverage is. If hearing is a priority for you or a family member, look specifically for plans with a strong hearing benefit when comparing during AEP.

Gym Memberships and Wellness Programs

Many Medicare Advantage plans in California include free access to gyms through programs like SilverSneakers, Renew Active (from UnitedHealthcare), or Silver&Fit. These programs offer:

  • Access to thousands of gym locations across the country
  • Fitness classes designed for older adults
  • Online wellness resources and mobile apps
  • Live and on-demand virtual classes

Regular exercise reduces the risk of falls, improves cardiovascular health, and supports mental well-being. For many older adults in California, having access to a quality gym at no additional cost is one of the highest-impact daily benefits a plan can offer.

Transportation to Medical Appointments

Some Medicare Advantage plans in California include a set number of non-emergency transportation rides per year to take you to medical appointments, labs, or pharmacies. This benefit is especially valuable for older adults who no longer drive or live in areas with limited public transit access.

The number of rides varies by plan, ranging from 12 to more than 50 per year. Some plans partner with transportation services like Lyft Health or MTM to coordinate rides.

Other Less-Known Benefits

Beyond the above, some Medicare Advantage plans in California also offer:

  • Flex Card: A prepaid card loaded with a set amount each year (from $500 to $1,500 on some plans) usable on approved health products, healthy foods, over-the-counter medications, or utility services.
  • Mental health support: Additional therapy or counseling sessions beyond what Original Medicare covers.
  • Chronic disease management programs: Additional support for people living with diabetes, heart failure, COPD, or other chronic conditions.
  • Telehealth visits: Virtual appointments with no copay or reduced copay.
  • Home care benefit: Some plans cover additional hours of in-home assistance beyond what Medicare covers, such as help with daily activities.

How to Find Out What Your Current Plan Covers

If you already have Medicare Advantage, the complete list of your benefits is in the Summary of Benefits your plan mailed when you enrolled. You can also:

  • Call the number on the back of your member card
  • Log into the plan's online member portal
  • Speak with a licensed agent who can review your plan with you

Many beneficiaries discover they have benefits they are not using, like the free gym membership or transportation to appointments, simply because they did not know they existed.

How to Find the Best Plan for Your Extra Benefits in California

The Annual Enrollment Period (AEP), October 15 through December 7, is the main window to compare and switch plans. To compare well:

  • Make a list of the extra benefits that matter most to you: expanded dental, hearing aids, gym access, transportation
  • Use the Medicare Plan Finder at Medicare.gov to see what plans are available in your California county
  • Compare not just the extra benefits but also the doctor network and the list of covered medications
  • Speak with a licensed agent who knows the plans in your county. The service is free to you.

Extra benefits vary considerably between counties. A plan available in Los Angeles may have a very different dental benefit than one available in Fresno or Sacramento. That is why comparing the specific plans in your area matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

What extra benefits do Medicare Advantage plans in California include?

Plans can include dental, vision, hearing, gym memberships, transportation to medical appointments, flex card benefits, telehealth, and more. The exact benefits depend on the plan and the county.

Do all Medicare Advantage plans include dental and vision?

Many do include at least basic preventive dental and a basic vision benefit. More comprehensive plans offer broader dental and hearing coverage. Compare plans in your county during AEP to find the one that best fits your needs.

What is SilverSneakers and is it available in California?

SilverSneakers is a wellness program offering older adults free gym access and fitness classes. Many Medicare Advantage plans in California include it. You can check your eligibility at silversneakers.com or by calling your plan.

Do extra benefits cost extra?

In most cases, no. Preventive dental, basic vision, gym memberships, and medical transportation are typically included in the plan's premium, which is often $0 per month. For more extensive services like crowns or premium hearing aids, cost-sharing or annual limits may apply. Always review the Summary of Benefits.

When can I enroll in a plan with these extra benefits?

The Annual Enrollment Period (AEP) runs October 15 through December 7. Changes take effect January 1. If you are new to Medicare, you have a 7-month Initial Enrollment Period. Special Enrollment Periods are available for qualifying life events.

Use What You Already Have

Extra Medicare Advantage benefits exist, but many beneficiaries simply do not use them. The dentist you have not seen in two years, the gym you did not know you could attend for free, the rides to medical appointments you never requested: these are benefits already included in your plan, even if your premium is $0.

Before AEP, it is worth reviewing what your current plan actually covers and comparing it to what other plans in your county offer. An informed change during the enrollment period can mean better benefits at the same or lower cost starting January 1.

Share this with someone you love. Many older adults do not realize their plan may already cover the dentist, eyeglasses, or a gym membership. Passing this along could help someone in your life make the most of what they already have.
