## Not done — found more structural drift than the ticket assumed, stopping to report rather than guess

The ticket treats MC's header/footer/head as roughly "one EN version + one ES version." That is not
what's actually on disk. Re-measured today, normalizing out only the known per-page variable (the
cross-language link href) and re-hashing:

- **Head (favicon + font links): 6 favicon-link variants, 10 font-link variants.** 24 of 94 pages
  have **no favicon `<link>` at all**. `404.html` itself has no Open Sans font link. This is the
  opposite of "already basically one file" — it's the least consistent of the three.
- **Header: 9 real structural variants**, not 2:
  - A 74-page majority (`sm-header sm-mc`, no SMS quick-link, `sm-burger` button) — most blog posts
    and `404.html`.
  - A 10-page variant (`benefits.html`, `best-medicare-advantage-california.html`,
    `dual-eligibility-medicare-medi-cal.html`, `how-to-enroll-medicare-california.html`,
    `medicare-costs-california.html`, `medicare-in-california.html` + 4 more) that adds an SMS
    quick-contact link (`t31-hcall`) and a different button class (`t31-menu`) — this is not an
    EN/ES split, both families contain only-EN pages; the split tracks **which pages got a later
    "add SMS shortcut" pass and which didn't**, independent of language.
  - **`index.html` and `es.html` (the two home pages) use a THIRD, deliberately different template**
    (`site-header t31-head`, `<nav class="site-nav t31-nav">`, anchor-style nav) — this is
    CLAUDE.md's documented single-page-site exception ("the Home/Benefits/About/Contact-style top nav
    belongs ONLY on the home page itself"). These must **not** be folded into the subpage template;
    they need their own `header-home-en.html` / `header-home-es.html`, out of this ticket's scope as
    written.
  - 5 more singleton pages (`beneficios.html`, `contacto.html`, `equipo.html` + the 2 homes) each
    carry their own small drift on top of one of the above families: a `data-cta="header"` attribute
    present on some cross-language links and not others, `sm-burger` vs `t31-menu` button markup,
    `t33-head` modifier class present or absent. None of this is a clean "majority wins" situation —
    each is a real, specific inconsistency that predates this ticket.
- **Footer: genuinely simpler** — confirmed only a **real language split** (87 EN pages, 5 ES pages:
  `beneficios.html contacto.html equipo.html es.html privacidad.html`). The 22 city-page "variant" I
  flagged for you to check turned out to be **not a content difference at all**: it's a stale staging-
  badge inline script that positions the badge `bottom-left` on city pages vs `top-right` everywhere
  else. That script only runs on `*.pages.dev` hosts (dead code on production), so folding city pages
  into the 65-page EN majority is safe for production and only changes a staging-only badge position.
  `footer-en.html` / `footer-es.html` are buildable with high confidence; I did not commit them yet
  because I'd rather you see the header finding first — same markers, same partial mechanism, trivial
  once header scope is decided.

**Why I stopped here instead of shipping a script against this:** a "majority wins" stamp across 9
header variants and 6 favicon variants, on a live Medicare site mid-AEP going straight to PRD today,
risks silently changing exactly the kind of thing CLAUDE.md's whole history here warns about — adding
or removing the SMS link on 10+ pages, or writing a favicon tag onto 24 pages that don't have one
today, is a real visible change, not cleanup, even though it looks like "just a logo file." The
ticket's own guardrail (6) anticipates this ("no visible-link change ships without AB seeing the
list") — I'd rather hand you that list before it's stamped, not after.

**Recommendation:** ship `logo.svg` + `brand-tokens.css` now (zero risk, confirmed byte-identical /
sourced from the live brand CSS). Treat the header/footer/head unification as needing one more
decision pass: (1) home pages keep their own template, always; (2) pick whether the 10-page
SMS-shortcut variant becomes the sitewide standard (added to the 74-page majority) or stays its own
listed exception; (3) decide the favicon/font gap (write the tags onto the 24/many pages missing them,
or leave as-is and just stop duplicating where they already match). None of these are mine to decide
silently on a live push.
